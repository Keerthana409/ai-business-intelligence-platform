# Frontend Pages

This directory contains route-level page views:
- `Login.jsx` & `Register.jsx`: Authentication pages
- `Dashboard.jsx`: Executive BI overview & KPI summary
- `SalesData.jsx`: Sales history, transactions, and CSV data ingestion
- `InventoryData.jsx`: Product stock levels, reorder alerts, and safety stock status
- `Forecast.jsx`: ML demand forecasting charts and comparisons
- `RiskAnalysis.jsx`: Stockout risk matrix, days of inventory, and ABC classification
- `WhatIfSimulation.jsx`: Interactive scenario simulator (price elasticity, lead time, promo campaigns)
- `Recommendations.jsx`: Actionable recommendations, rationale inspection, and outcome tracking
